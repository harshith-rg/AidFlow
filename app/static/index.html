About this demo

  • Demo data. Hospital names, bed counts, specialties and update times are fictional and exist only for testing. They are not real hospital availability.
  • Estimated ETA. ETA is the road distance divided by an assumed average speed. Live traffic is not used.
  • No tracking, no dispatch. The route preview moves a marker along the route line the service returned. It is a simulation, not the position of any vehicle, and nothing is sent to any hospital or crew.
  • Coverage. Routing uses the loaded Bengaluru road map. Hospitals outside it are skipped by the service, and the Hospitals page lists only those inside it.
  • Bed data age. A hospital whose bed data is older than the service's stale limit is still eligible, but its bed counts are ignored and it is marked "bed data may be stale".
